---
title: "copying technology makes you fall behind"
claim: "Delegating the R&D process to catch up faster on cutting edge technologies will leave you permanently chasing instead of bringing you to the leading pack."
sources: ["chip-war"]
topics: []
---

Soviet technological research programs mostly failed because they tried to delegate the R&D part to the USA and simply copy the final technology, with the intent of catching up by cutting corners and then investing only once they were on the same level; this largely failed because the plan was missing fundamental ingredients of the innovation process.
