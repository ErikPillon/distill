## Notes

- The history of chips it the story of how a country with a technological advantege obtained through military fundings shaped international geopolitical dynamics eventually leading to the biggest chokepoint in history.
- Even if the syslicon valley is not anymore the leading chip-producing region in the world, eveything still relies on software and customers that belongs in the sylicon valley
- Silicon Valley=Military Need + California Know how + entrpreneurial/talent attraction mindset
- Most successfull innovations were born through the upgrade to general purpose technologies from specific problems solutions.
- Most of the history of chip development is an engineering problem, not a physics one (refining processes and trial-error were more important than costly inventing new technologies)
- Chip development followed since the early beginnings an exponential growth and this law proved inexorable over time (=> Wright law?)
- "Catching up and overtaking " startegy condemned non-innovative countries to always fall behind. Enterpreneurial, collaborative and disruptive spirits proved necessary to build world class sustainable ecosystems
- The need to preserve technological supremacy helped maintaining military advantage and viceversa
- technological collaboration agreements shape geopolitical alliences and tensions.
