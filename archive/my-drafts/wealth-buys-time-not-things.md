---
title: "Wealth is control of time"
claim: "The real return on wealth is paid in autonomy: the point of accumulating is to own your time, not to own more."
sources: ["psychology-money"]
locator: "ch. 7"
topics: ["wealth", "happiness"]

Stated as a test rather than a sentiment: if a marginal unit of money does not buy back a unit of control over your day, it is not doing the job you accumulated it for.
